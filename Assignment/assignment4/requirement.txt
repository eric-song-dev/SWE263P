Introduction
Two weeks ago you developed a persona and journey map for the fictional UXAirways. In this assignment, you will develop the visual design for their mobile app. This will include creating a low- to medium-fidelity wireframe of the app, and a high-fidelity mockup/interactive Figma prototype.

The goals of this assignment are to:

demonstrate your understanding of UX principles (e.g. providing good signifiers and feedback), visual design principles (e.g. visual hierarchy),
demonstrate your ability to communicate designs in both low-fidelity and high-fidelity genres, and
demonstrate your ability to use a state-of-the-art design tool (Figma).
While beauty is appreciated, we will not be grading the aesthetics of your design.

Design Brief
Your task is to come up with a design for the UXAirways mobile app. You should use the persona and journey map that you already created to help shape the design of your app.

Your app must include at least 8 different pages/screens. You do not need to design all of the functionality of the app, or design all your features in depth. You can also include signifiers for features that are not yet developed. For example, your home page might have an icon to adjust app settings, even though you didn't design the settings page.

Do not design a login page - you should assume the user is already logged in when they open your app.

Your design must include:

At least one multi-page user flow. For example, getting a flight status might include 3 pages after the Home Screen: Home Screen -> Search Flights -> Choose Flight -> View Status
At least one scrollable list/page (horizontal or vertical)
At least one pop-up or modal dialog box
You are not required to include iPhone/Android standard information & interactions at the top and bottom of the screen, but you might find them helpful (e.g. having a "back" option can help your navigation). If you do include these, you should be sure to follow iOSLinks to an external site. or AndroidLinks to an external site. design standards.

You will be developing this app in two versions:

Wireframe: This version should indicate the basic layout and functionality without any of the "look and feel" aspects. Specifically:
Use greeked textLinks to an external site. for any informational content (e.g. flight numbers, advertising text, descriptions, etc.)
Use only black, white, and 1 shade of gray (at most). Do not use gradients.
Use only basic shapes (e.g. rectangles and circles) without significant decorations
Use simple B&W icons (if any)
The wireframe does not need to be interactive (but can be if you want)
High Fidelity Interactive Prototype
This should show the exact same screens as your wireframe
Layouts of screens should be basically the same
Use color, styles, images, etc. to provide a sense of the look and feel of the app
Replace greeked text with representative real text
Use the Figma prototyping tools to allow the user to navigate your app, and use high-fidelity interactions (dragging, pop-ups, etc.) where appropriate
Because your app must include 8 pages, and you must create 2 versions, your final deliverable will have a minimum of 16 pages. (Dialog boxes and similar pop-up content do not add to your page count.) Be sure your Figma file is arranged clearly.

It's OK to use a Figma kit (with citation in your Figma file) to help with your wireframing and provide various placeholders, components, and icons. Be sure to use one that is not too fancy or complex—wireframes are meant to be graphically straightforward and simple. I often use Nailul Izah's Free Wireframes KitLinks to an external site. or Tiago Gonçalves' Wireframing Starter KitLinks to an external site..

Additional Information
You are free to use tutorials and examples from online sources (the Figma tutorial videos are quite good). I strongly advise you to check out other airline apps to see what kinds of functionality are typical (this is called "competitive analysis"). You may also use design kits like the Free Wireframes KitLinks to an external site. (with citation in your Figma file). However, plagiarizing your design from another source is not allowed. Your design must be your own.
Generative AI use is not allowed for wireframing, laying out your mockup, or creating your interactive prototypes. You may use generative AI to create component graphics for your high-fidelity mockup if you wish, but you must indicate that you did so, and the overall design must be your own.
Feel free to add annotations or comments to explain what you did and why.

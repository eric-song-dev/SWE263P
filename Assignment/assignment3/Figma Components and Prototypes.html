Design Specification

This assignment will give you experience using Figma to create an interactive prototype.

In this assignment you will build an interactive prototype of a survey to be taken on a mobile phone. The survey should present only 1 question per page. Users should be able to navigate both forward and backward through the survey. Component states (e.g. survey answers) should be preserved when navigating away from and then back to a page.

The survey should contain the following pages/questions:

Survey question requirements
Page# Question Answer type Required interaction
1 An introduction page. This page should have a title and your name as author. It should then have text (like instructions or terms of use) that must be seen before beginning the survey. There must be enough text that the user must scroll down to see it all. The text doesn't matter - feel free to use a Lorem Ipsum generator Links to an external site. or copy random text.
2 What is your favorite name? Short-answer text box Clicking on box will show it filled.
3 What is your favorite date? Date text box Clicking on date field will show a drop-down date picker calendar widget. Clicking anywhere on the calendar will fill a pre-determined date into the date text box and hide the calendar widget. You do not need to make the calendar itself interactive (e.g. hover effects on individual days or the ability to switch months). For this component, you may copy an image of a calendar rather than creating your own, but you must create the text box and all interactions from scratch.
4 What is your favorite primary color? (Choose one.) Radio buttons to choose Red, Green, or Blue. On first load, no radio button should be selected. On each click of the component, it should cycle through which choice is selected. Optional advanced mode: allow clicking on individual choices while maintaining the "choose only one" constraint.
5 Which meals do you typically eat in a day? (Select all that apply.) Checkboxes to choose Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner. On first load no checkboxes should be selected. Clicking any checkbox should toggle its state between unselected and selected.
6 Are you ready to submit your survey? Back button and Submit button On clicking the Submit button, a modal popup box should appear asking the user to confirm their choice. If they confirm, they should be taken to a page that confirms the submission was successful and thanks them.

Figma Instructions

Start with a new blank Figma design file to work with.

The file should have 2 pages: a "Components" page where you develop the individual components that you will use in your design, and a "Survey" page where you lay out the screens of your design.

All interactive elements (buttons, input fields and widgets, etc.) must be created as reusable components. It is up to you if you want to create components for pure text elements (like Question text). Components should allow as realistic interaction as possible. Anything that a user would expect to be able to click should be clickable. Buttons should have hover interactions to show that they are clickable.

Ensure that the names of objects (frames, components, etc.) are sensible.

The Survey page should be arranged somewhat like a storyboard (e.g. progress through the app reads left-to-right and/or top-to-bottom) so that the entire app can be visualized at once.

You will be graded primarily on the ability to create an interactive prototype in Figma, but you should follow good UX principles. Feel free to use color or simple graphics, but you will not be graded on the quality of your graphic design.

Additional Information

  • You are free to use tutorials and examples from online sources (the Figma tutorial videos are quite good). However, importing or copying and pasting any aspect of your design from another source is not allowed. You may not use plugins or design kits. Unless specified, all work must be your own and must be created from scratch.
  • Not every type of interaction is easily supported in Figma. For example, it's not really supported to have a text box that accepts user input or does input validation. Read the interaction requirements carefully - they have been written in a way that is possible to do in Figma.
  • Feel free to add annotations or comments to explain what you did and why.
  • You may not use generative AI (including Figma Make) for this assignment.

Submitting Your Assignment

  1. In your Figma Design File, click the "Share" button.
  2. Add mbietz@uci.edu and yihungc1@uci.edu as viewers to your file.
  3. Copy the sharing link and paste it here as your submission.
